\section{Background}
\label{sec:background}

\paragraph{EO scheduling.}
Greedy dispatch rules suit onboard use~\citep{bianchessi_heuristic_2007, bianchessi_planning_2008, wolfe_three_2000}, while MILPs optimize known utility~\citep{bensana_earth_1999, augenstein_optimal_2016, nag_autonomous_2019}.
Graph formulations exploit sparse access feasibility for cheaper near-optimal solutions~\citep{augenstein_optimal_2014, eddy_maximum_2021, eddy_task_2021}; MDP, reinforcement-learning, and Monte Carlo tree search methods avoid the access-based formulation~\citep{eddy_markov_2020, herrmann_reinforcement_2023, wang_deep_2023, hadj-salah_schedule_2019, herrmann_monte_2022}.

These methods decide how to optimize a known set of accesses, but the central obstacle for dynamic tasking is not only schedule optimization: it is deciding which uncertain future accesses are worth observing before the primary instrument commits.

\paragraph{Dynamic targeting and tasking.}
Dynamic \emph{targeting} acquires information and creates targets within an overflight; dynamic \emph{tasking} repairs an initial schedule over fixed requests. EO-1 pioneered onboard replanning and ML classification, although limited compute restricted it mainly to follow-up observations~\citep{chien_using_2005, chien_lessons_2005, wagstaff_cloud_2017}. GOSAT-2 now redirects its primary instrument from clouds with a fold mirror~\citep{imasu_greenhouse_2023}, and CogniSAT-6 demonstrated CNN-based autonomous tasking on a 6U CubeSat~\citep{rijlaarsdam_autonomous_2024}.

Onboard replanning has used anytime planners, timeline fragments, pointing heuristics, constellation-aware MILPs, and MDPs~\citep{damiani_continuous_2005, lenzen_onboard_2014, chien_heuristic_nodate, chien_onboard_2015, nag_autonomous_2019, candela_dynamic_2022}.
The closest comparator is \citet{kangaslahti_dynamic_2024}, which searches lookahead choices under slew and changing-utility constraints.
Its different observation model lacks our body-fixed geometry, pitch-dependent support, and anchor-bounded repair, precluding a direct numerical comparison.
These methods establish that onboard adaptation is possible, but generally search directly over sensing actions or assume a fixed observation strategy rather than first deriving the lookahead decision space from spacecraft constraints.
The remaining gap is a computationally lightweight value model for body-fixed lookahead that connects geometry and agility to schedule-level benefit and formal timing guarantees.
We address that gap and compare against conventional and omniscient bounds plus a greedy policy in the same simulator.

\paragraph{Onboard compute and agility.}
\label{sec:compute}
\label{sec:agility_background}
EO-1's ${\sim}4$~MIPS shared processor could not analyze lookahead imagery within an overflight~\citep{chien_using_2005, chien_lessons_2005}; recent COTS VPU/GPU-class processors can~\citep{kacker_machine_2022, giuffrida_-sat-1_2022, marin_phi-sat-2_2021, rijlaarsdam_autonomous_2024, rijlaarsdam_next_2024, noauthor_planet_2024}.

Agility determines whether lookahead information remains actionable~\citep{eddy_task_2021, lemaitre_selecting_2002}. Representative platforms offer $\pm$\ang{45} off-nadir and complete an extreme slew in about 25--40~s; Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix_system_data} summarizes public specifications, and \secref{sec:lookahead_design} derives the coupled constraints.

\paragraph{Sequential information acquisition.}
Dynamic tasking is sequential information acquisition: the spacecraft spends resources to reveal task values before committing its primary instrument. Related robotics methods value uncertainty reduction or coverage under travel, time, or energy constraints~\citep{singh_nonmyopic_2009, tan_informative_2024}; inspection and probing models value information but usually assume separable items~\citep{weitzman_optimal_1978, gupta_algorithms_2015}. Here a revealed target matters only if it fits a feasible schedule, coupling information value to agility and repair.
